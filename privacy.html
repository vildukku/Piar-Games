Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

Overview

Welcome to Bus Tracker App (developed by Piar Games). Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application operates and handles user information when you use our mobile application.

Key Takeaway: We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share any of your personal identification data. Your location is processed solely in real-time to show your distance from the approaching bus.

Location Data Usage

To provide core bus tracking functionality, our application requests access to your device's Precise or Approximate Location.

  • Purpose of Access: Location permission is used strictly to calculate the real-time distance between your current position and the selected bus or bus stop.
  • Real-time Processing: Your location coordinates are processed temporarily in-memory on your device only while using the active tracking features.
  • No Location Storage: Your location data is never saved to external databases, never logged across time, and never transmitted to third parties.

No Personal Data Collection

We believe in absolute data minimization. The Bus Tracker App does not require user accounts, registrations, or logins.

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
  • We do not collect financial, payment, or identity information.
  • We do not build user profiles or track your movements across other apps.

Device Permissions

The app requires the following standard Android permissions to function properly:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Used exclusively for calculating your distance to approaching buses.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to retrieve live public transit bus position data from server endpoints.

Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for general audiences of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, children can safely use the app to track local public transport without privacy concerns.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically if new transit features are introduced. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with an updated effective date.
